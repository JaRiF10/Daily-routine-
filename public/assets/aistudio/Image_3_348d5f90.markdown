## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a focused, calm, and distraction-free productivity environment. Tailored for knowledge workers, developers, and product teams, it reduces cognitive friction while reinforcing momentum and accomplishment through intentional visual feedback.

The aesthetic blends **Elevated Minimalism** with structured workspace utility:
- **Calm & Measured:** Generous negative space, restrained tonal shifts, and an absence of loud visual noise promote prolonged cognitive stamina.
- **Micro-Delight through Precision:** Hairline borders, precise typographic alignment, and gentle status shifts celebrate progress without overwhelming the user.
- **Warm Neutrality:** Instead of stark, clinical coldness, light surfaces lean on balanced zinc and warm off-white backdrops to keep interfaces natural and human.

## Colors

The palette employs a grounded balance between quiet neutrals and high-intent semantic accents:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas uses off-white `#fafafa`, transitioning to pure white `#ffffff` for elevated cards and modals to create distinct focal planes.
  - Secondary containers, subtle table headers, and inactive states sit on tinted zinc neutrals (`#f4f4f5` to `#e4e4e7`).
- **Primary Accent (`#4f46e5` - Indigo):** Reserved for core interactive triggers, focused inputs, active filter states, and primary CTAs.
- **Success Accent (`#059669` - Emerald):** Deployed for completed tasks, milestone progress bars, and affirmative confirmations.
- **Destructive Accent (`#e11d48` - Rose):** Applied selectively to task deletion, critical warnings, and overdue indicators to ensure immediate recognition without harsh alarmism.
- **Neutrals & Text:** Primary body and headings leverage deep slate/zinc `#18181b` for maximum contrast and legibility, while metadata, timestamps, and secondary captions sit at `#71717a`.

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Geist** across all roles, capitalizing on its structural neutrality, modern geometric clarity, and high data density suitability:

- **Hierarchy & Rhythm:** Tightly-tracked headings (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) ground views with intentional editorial weight, while body text maintains neutral tracking to optimize multi-line scanning across descriptions and task logs.
- **Contextual Roles:** `label-xs` is uppercase-tracked for project tags, keyboard shortcuts, and meta chips. `body-sm` handles secondary details such as due dates and subtask counters without visually conflicting with parent task titles.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8-point base grid drives spacing decisions, enhanced with a 4-point micro-scale (`space-xs`) for dense productivity UI elements (badges, nested list markers, and input paddings):

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (≥1024px):** Fixed left navigation (240px) or collapsible rail (64px) with a fluid multi-column workspace or Kanban board utilizing `gutter-md` (1.5rem) and outer `margin-md` (2rem). Content containers cap at max-width 1280px for standard list views to prevent awkward horizontal drift.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid single/double column with collapsible panels; 1rem gutters and 1.5rem margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stack, edge-to-edge scroll zones, bottom action sheets, and tight 1rem margins.
- **Micro-Spacing:** Gaps within task row components adhere strictly to `space-sm` (8px) between checkboxes and text, and `space-md` (12px) between card sections, ensuring tightly grouped information hierarchy.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on **low-contrast outlines complemented by subtle ambient shadows**:

- **Borders over Mass:** Structural definition is primary achieved via a crisp 1px hairline border (`#e4e4e7` / Zinc 200). In dark-tinted or active states, borders step to `#d4d4d8` (Zinc 300).
- **Ambient Shadow Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Flat):** Flush layout blocks and static lists rely solely on surface tinting and hairline dividers.
  - **Level 1 (Card / Resting):** `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.03)` with a 1px border.
  - **Level 2 (Hover / Active Drag):** `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04)`. Card border transitions to an indigo-tinted slate.
  - **Level 3 (Overlay / Menus / Modals):** `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Soft roundedness (`roundedness: 1`)** to produce an engineered, functional environment that feels precise rather than toy-like:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Form controls, text inputs, badges, task checkboxes, and tooltips.
- **Medium Radius (`rounded-lg` - 0.5rem / 8px):** Task cards, dropdown sheets, contextual menus, and interactive list groups.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-xl` - 0.75rem / 12px):** Global modals, drawer panels, and elevated floating command palettes (e.g., Quick Add / Search `Cmd+K`).

## Components

### Task Cards
- **Container:** Pure white `#ffffff` background with 1px border (`#e4e4e7`), 8px radius (`rounded-lg`), resting Level 1 shadow.
- **Padding:** 12px vertical by 16px horizontal for standard lists; 16px uniform for Kanban boards.
- **States:** Hover brings a 1px border transition to `#d4d4d8` and Level 2 elevation. Completed tasks trigger a smooth cross-fade: text transitions to `line-through` with `#a1a1aa`, while the container reduces opacity to `0.75`.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** `#4f46e5` background, white text, 4px radius, 0.5px subtle top inner highlight. Hover: `#4338ca`. Active: slight 0.98 scale compression.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px border (`#e4e4e7`), `#18181b` text. Hover: `#f4f4f5` background.
- **Destructive:** Subtle rose wash (`#fff1f2`), `#e11d48` text and border. Hover: `#ffe4e6`.

### Checkboxes
- **Frame:** 18x18px square with 4px border-radius, 1.5px border (`#d4d4d8`).
- **Checked (Complete):** Animated background fill to emerald `#059669`, white SVG checkmark scaled from center, border becomes `#059669`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Tag Style:** Height of 22px, padding 0 8px, `label-xs` typography, 4px radius.
- **Variants:** Neutral (`#f4f4f5` bg, `#52525b` text), Priority High (`#fff1f2` bg, `#e11d48` text), Completed (`#ecfdf5` bg, `#059669` text).

### Form Inputs & Quick-Add
- **Input Fields:** 36px height, pure white surface, 1px border (`#e4e4e7`), inset shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.02)`. Focus: border switches to `#4f46e5` with a 2px outer glow (`rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`).
- **Inline Quick Add:** Minimalist input seamlessly embedded at the base of task lists, activated via `Enter` to create rapid low-friction capture loops.

### Lists & Drag Handlers
- **Groupings:** Separated by sticky section headers featuring uppercase `label-xs` typography and subtle item count pills.
- **Reordering:** Subtle six-dot drag indicator (`#a1a1aa`) revealed on row hover, triggering Level 2 elevation and a slight tilt during repositioning.
